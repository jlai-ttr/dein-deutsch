VERB "HABEN" — TO HAVE
----------------------
This is your second most important verb. Used for:
- Possession: "I have a car"
- Age: "I am 30 years old" (literally: "I have 30 years")
- Many idiomatic expressions

  ich habe        = I have          = ISH HAH-beh
  du hast         = you have (cas)  = DOO HAST
  Sie haben       = you have (fml)  = ZEE HAH-ben
  er hat          = he has          = AIR HAT
  sie hat         = she has         = ZEE HAT
  es hat          = it has          = ESS HAT
  wir haben       = we have         = VEER HAH-ben
  ihr habt        = you (pl) have   = EER HAHPT
  sie haben       = they have       = ZEE HAH-ben

Memory trick: "habe/hast/hat/hat/hat" for singular,
"haben/habt/haben" for plural + formal.

EXAMPLES WITH "HABEN"
--------------------
  Ich habe ein Auto.              = I have a car.
  Du hast eine Schwester.         = You have a sister. (casual)
  Sie haben Zeit.                 = You have time. (formal)
  Er hat zwei Brüder.             = He has two brothers.
  Wir haben keine Kinder.         = We have no children.
  Sie haben ein Meeting um 10.    = They have a meeting at 10.

AGE — IMPORTANT PATTERN
------------------------
In English: "I am 30 years old"
In German: "Ich habe 30 Jahre." (I have 30 years)

  Ich bin 30 Jahre alt.     ← standard
  Ich habe 30 Jahre.        ← also correct, more literal

  "Wie alt bist du?" = How old are you? (casual)
  "Wie alt sind Sie?" = How old are you? (formal)
  "Ich bin 30." or "Ich habe 30 Jahre."

GENDER + ARTICLES — DER/DIE/DAS (THE BIG ONE)
---------------------------------------------
German nouns have 3 genders. Every noun has a gender. Memorize the
gender WITH the noun. This is critical — affects every other part of
speech.

  der = masculine (m)         = DAIR
  die = feminine (f)          = DEE
  das = neuter (n)            = DAHS

Examples:
  der Mann (the man) — m
  die Frau (the woman) — f
  das Kind (the child) — n
  der Tisch (the table) — m
  die Lampe (the lamp) — f
  das Buch (the book) — n
  der Stuhl (the chair) — m
  die Tür (the door) — f

INDEFINITE ARTICLE: "EIN/EINE" (A / AN)
---------------------------------------
  ein = a/an (for masculine + neuter)
  eine = a/an (for feminine)

  ein Mann (a man) — m
  ein Kind (a child) — n
  eine Frau (a woman) — f
  ein Auto (a car) — n
  eine Lampe (a lamp) — f

Memory: "eine" ends in -e like "die" (feminine).

THE "NO" FORM: KEIN/KEINE
-------------------------
  kein = no/not a (for masculine + neuter)
  keine = no/not a (for feminine + plural)

  Ich habe kein Auto.     = I have no car.
  Sie hat keine Kinder.   = She has no children.
  Wir haben kein Geld.    = We have no money.

FAMILY VOCABULARY — DIE FAMILIE
-------------------------------
  die Familie       = the family
  der Vater         = the father    = FAH-ter
  die Mutter        = the mother   = MOO-ter
  der Bruder        = the brother  = BROO-der
  die Schwester     = the sister   = SHVES-ter
  der Sohn          = the son      = ZOHN
  die Tochter       = the daughter = TOKH-ter
  der Großvater     = the grandfather
  die Großmutter    = the grandmother
  der Onkel         = the uncle
  die Tante         = the aunt
  der Cousin        = the cousin (male)
  die Cousine       = the cousin (female)
  das Kind          = the child
  die Kinder        = the children (plural)
  der Mann          = the man/husband
  die Frau          = the woman/wife
  der Freund        = the friend (male)
  die Freundin      = the friend (female)

  "Mein" (my):
  mein Vater (m)    = my father
  meine Mutter (f)  = my mother
  mein Kind (n)     = my child
  meine Kinder (pl) = my children

  "Ich habe einen Bruder und eine Schwester."
  = ISH HAH-beh AYN-en BROO-der oont AY-neh SHVES-ter
  (I have a brother and a sister.)

30% BUSINESS LAYER — DAY 4
---------------------------
  "Haben Sie Zeit?" = HAH-ben ZEE TSAYT?
    (Do you have time? — formal)

  "Ich habe heute keine Zeit." = ISH HAH-beh HOY-teh KAY-neh TSAYT
    (I have no time today.)

  "Haben Sie Fragen?" = HAH-ben ZEE FRAH-gen?
    (Do you have questions?)

  "Ich habe eine Frage." = ISH HAH-beh AY-neh FRAH-geh
    (I have a question.)

  "Wir haben ein Problem." = VEER HAH-ben AYN proh-BLAYM
    (We have a problem.)

  "Das Hotel hat 50 Zimmer." = DAS ho-TEL HAT FOONF-tsich TSIM-mer
    (The hotel has 50 rooms.)

  "Haben Sie einen Termin?" = HAH-ben ZEE AYN-en TERM-een?
    (Do you have an appointment?)

  "Ich habe morgen einen Termin."
  = ISH HAH-beh MOR-gen AYN-en TERM-een
  (I have an appointment tomorrow.)

  "Wir haben eine Besprechung um 10 Uhr."
  = VEER HAH-ben AY-neh beh-SHPRAY-khoong OOM TSAYN OOR
  (We have a meeting at 10 o'clock.)

   a) Ich habe _____ Auto.          (a car)

   c) Wir haben _____ Kinder.       (no children)
   d) Er hat _____ Schwester.       (a sister)

   f) Ich habe _____ Frage.         (a question)

3. Translate:
   a) I have a brother.              = ________________
   b) Do you (formal) have time?     = ________________
   c) We have a meeting at 10.       = ________________
   d) She has no children.           = ________________
   e) My father is 60 years old.     = ________________
   f) Do you have questions?         = ________________

ANSWERS
=======

   d) haben   e) haben   f) haben

2. a) ein     b) einen   c) keine
   d) eine    e) keine   f) eine

3. a) Ich habe einen Bruder.
   b) Haben Sie Zeit?
