PRONUNCIATION CHEAT SHEET
-------------------------
German pronunciation is REGULAR — once you learn the rules, you read
anything correctly. Unlike English.

1. "W" = English "V" sound
   "Wasser" (water) = "VAH-ser"
   "Wir" (we) = "VEER"

2. "V" = English "F" sound (in German words)
   "Vater" (father) = "FAH-ter"

3. "Z" = "TS" sound
   "Zug" (train) = "TZOOG"
   "Zeit" (time) = "TSAIT"

4. "S" before a vowel = English "Z" sound
   "Sonne" (sun) = "ZON-neh"

5. "SCH" = English "SH" sound
   "Schule" (school) = "SHOO-leh"

6. "CH" after a/o/u = guttural "KH" (like clearing your throat softly)
   "Buch" (book) = "BOOKH"
   "CH" after e/i = lighter "SH" sound
   "ich" (I) = "ISH"

7. "EI" = English "EYE"
   "Klein" (small) = "KLAYNE"

8. "EU" / "ÄU" = English "OY"
   "Deutsch" (German) = "DOYTSH"
   "Häuser" (houses) = "HOY-zer"

9. "J" = English "Y"
   "Ja" (yes) = "YAH"

10. "R" = rolled or guttural, never English "R"
    "Rot" (red) = soft "ROOT"

11. Final "D" or "B" = softer "T" or "P"
    "Hund" (dog) = "HOONT"

12. Stress is usually on the FIRST syllable
    "VAt-er", "MUt-ter", "Kin-der"

GREETINGS — DIE BEGRÜSSUNGEN
----------------------------
Casual (with friends, peers, family):
  Hallo!           = HEL-low         (Hello / Hi)
  Hi!              = HEE             (Hi)
  Servus!          = ZER-voos        (Hi — Southern Germany, Austria)

Formal / business (use these in meetings, with strangers, in shops):
  Guten Morgen!    = GOO-ten MOR-gen (Good morning — until ~noon)
  Guten Tag!       = GOO-ten TAHK    (Good day — safest all-day option)
  Guten Abend!     = GOO-ten AH-bent (Good evening — after 6pm)
  Grüß Gott!       = GROOSS GOT      (Greeting — Bavaria/Austria, formal)

VERY COMMON:
  Wie geht's?      = VEE GAYTS       (How's it going? — casual)
  Wie geht es Ihnen? = VEE GAYT es EE-nen  (How are you? — formal)

RESPONSES:
  Gut, danke.      = GOOT, DAN-keh   (Good, thanks.)
  Sehr gut.        = ZAYR GOOT       (Very good.)
  Es geht.         = ESS GAYT        (It's okay.)
  Nicht so gut.    = NISHT zo GOOT   (Not so good.)
  Und dir/Ihnen?   = OONT DEER/EE-nen  (And you? — casual/formal)

SAYING GOODBYE:
  Tschüss!         = CHOOSS          (Bye — casual)
  Auf Wiedersehen! = OWF VEE-der-zayn  (Goodbye — formal)
  Bis später!      = BIS SHAY-ter    (See you later)
  Bis morgen!      = BIS MOR-gen     (See you tomorrow)
  Bis dann!        = BIS DAN         (See you then)
  Gute Nacht!      = GOO-teh NAKHT   (Good night)

INTRODUCING YOURSELF
--------------------
  Ich heiße...     = ISH HAY-seh     (My name is...)
    "Ich heiße Jasper" = "ISH HAY-seh Jasper"
  Ich bin...        = ISH BIN         (I am...)
    "Ich bin Jasper" = "ISH BIN Jasper"
  Wie heißt du?    = VEE HAYST DOO   (What's your name? — casual)
  Wie heißen Sie?   = VEE HAY-sen ZEE (What's your name? — formal)

  Woher kommen Sie? = VOH-HAIR KOM-men ZEE (Where are you from? — formal)
  Ich komme aus Malaysia. = ISH KOM-meh AOWS Ma-lay-zia
  Ich komme aus Kuala Lumpur. = ISH KOM-meh AOWS KWAH-lah LOOM-poor

  Wie alt bist du? = VEE ALT BIST DOO  (How old are you? — casual)
  Ich bin 30 Jahre alt. = ISH BIN DRAY-sik YAH-reh ALT

PLEASE / THANK YOU / YOU'RE WELCOME
-----------------------------------
  Bitte.           = BIT-teh         (Please / You're welcome / Here you go)
  Danke.           = DAN-keh         (Thanks)
  Vielen Dank.     = FEE-len DANK    (Many thanks)
  Danke schön.     = DAN-keh SHOON   (Thanks very much)
  Danke sehr.      = DAN-keh ZAYR    (Thanks very much — formal)
  Gerne.           = GER-neh         (You're welcome — "gladly")

  Ja.   = YAH    (Yes)
  Nein. = NAYNE  (No)
  Vielleicht. = FIL-LAYSH-t   (Maybe)

30% BUSINESS LAYER — DAY 1
---------------------------
Business opening lines for emails/meetings:
  Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, = ZAYR geh-AYR-teh DAH-men oont HERR-en
    (Dear Sir/Madam — formal letter opening, no name known)
  Sehr geehrte Frau [Name],       = ZAYR geh-AYR-teh FROW [Name]
    (Dear Mrs. [Name])
  Sehr geehrter Herr [Name],      = ZAYR geh-AYR-ter HERR [Name]
    (Dear Mr. [Name])

  Mit freundlichen Grüßen = MIT FROYND-lich-en GROO-sen
    (Kind regards — standard email sign-off)

  Guten Tag, Herr Ting. = GOO-ten TAHK, HERR Ting
    (Good day, Mr. Ting — formal address with surname + Herr)

NOTE: In business, ALWAYS use "Sie" (formal "you") + surname until invited
to use "du". The "du" + first name switch is a milestone in German business
culture.
