GERMAN A1 — DAY 10 OF 90
Numbers 1-100 + Telling time
=============================

You've made it 10 days! Numbers are critical — for prices, addresses,
phone numbers, dates, and time.

NUMBERS 1-20 (memorize these first)
------------------------------------

 1 = eins          11 = elf
 2 = zwei          12 = zwölf
 3 = drei          13 = dreizehn
 4 = vier          14 = vierzehn
 5 = fünf          15 = fünfzehn  (note: f not ff — five shifts spelling)
 6 = sechs         16 = sechzehn  (note: s not sz — six drops the 's' from sechs)
 7 = sieben        17 = siebzehn  (note: drops the second 'e' from sieben)
 8 = acht          18 = achtzehn
 9 = neun          19 = neunzehn
10 = zehn          20 = zwanzig

Watch out! These are irregular:
  eins, zwei, drei, vier, fünf, sechs, sieben, acht, neun, zehn
  elf, zwölf (12 has its own form)
  Numbers 13-19 = (3-9) + zehn

NUMBERS 21-99 (pattern: ones + und + tens)
-------------------------------------------

21 = einundzwanzig (one and twenty) — yes, ones come first!
32 = zweiunddreißig (two and thirty)
43 = dreiundvierzig (three and forty)
99 = neunundneunzig (nine and ninety)

Notice: "dreißig" (30) has ß, not ss. And the order is BACKWARDS from English.

100 = hundert (or "einhundert" formally)
200 = zweihundert
1000 = tausend (or "eintausend" formally)

TELLING TIME
------------

Two ways — official (24-hour) and conversational (12-hour).

Official (used for timetables, schedules):
  08:00 = acht Uhr
  14:30 = vierzehn Uhr dreißig
  19:45 = neunzehn Uhr fünfundvierzig

Conversational (most common in speech):
  "Wie spät ist es?" (What time is it?)

  Full hour:
    08:00 = acht Uhr (8 o'clock)
    14:00 = vierzehn Uhr / zwei Uhr nachmittags (2 PM)

  Half hour:
    08:30 = halb neun (half nine — yes, "half nine" means 8:30!)
    Why? Because it's "half way TO nine o'clock." Mind-bending.

  Quarter hours:
    08:15 = Viertel nach acht (quarter past eight)
    08:45 = Viertel vor neun (quarter to nine)

  5-minute increments:
    08:05 = fünf nach acht (5 past 8)
    08:55 = fünf vor neun (5 to 9)

  Special word: "Viertel" means "quarter" (15 min).
  "Viertel nach" = past. "Viertel vor" = to.

DAYS OF THE WEEK (Wochentage)
------------------------------
 Monday    = Montag
 Tuesday   = Dienstag
 Wednesday = Mittwoch
 Thursday  = Donnerstag (literally "thunder day")
 Friday    = Freitag
 Saturday  = Samstag / Sonnabend
 Sunday    = Sonntag

Note: All days are masculine "der". The week starts on Monday in Germany.

MONTHS (Monate)
---------------
 January   = Januar       July      = Juli
 February  = Februar      August    = August
 March     = März (note: ß)  September = September
 April     = April        October   = Oktober
 May       = Mai          November  = November
 June      = Juni         December  = Dezember
