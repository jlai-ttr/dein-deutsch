TEMPORAL PREPOSITIONS
----------------------

These are prepositions that deal with TIME:

1. AN (on — days, dates)

   "Am Montag" (on Monday)
   "Am 15. März" (on 15 March)
   "Am Wochenende" (on the weekend)

   AN + der = am
   "an dem Tag" = "am Tag"
   "an dem Wochenende" = "am Wochenende"

2. IN (in — months, years, seasons, parts of day)

   "Im Januar" (in January)
   "Im Jahr 2026" (in 2026)
   "Im Sommer" (in summer)
   "Im Frühling/Herbst/Winter" (in spring/fall/winter)
   "Im 20. Jahrhundert" (in the 20th century)
   "Morgens/Mittags/Abends/Nachts" (in the morning/noon/evening/night — these are adverbs formed from "der Morgen" etc. + -s)

   Note: "Morgens" (in the morning) is just an adverb, no preposition needed. Common in spoken German.

3. UM (at — clock time)

   "Um 8 Uhr" (at 8 o'clock)
   "Um welche Uhrzeit?" (At what time?)
   "Um Viertel nach acht" (at quarter past eight)

4. VOR (before)

   "Vor einer Stunde" (an hour ago / one hour before)
   "Vor drei Tagen" (three days ago)
   "Vor dem Essen" (before eating)

   "vor" + dative
   "Vor drei Jahren" = three years ago

5. NACH (after)

   "Nach dem Essen" (after eating)
   "Nach einer Stunde" (after an hour)
   "Nach der Arbeit" (after work)

   "nach" + dative

6. SEIT (since / for — ongoing from past)

   "Seit drei Jahren" (for three years / since three years)
   "Seit wann?" (Since when?)
   "Seit gestern" (since yesterday)

   "seit" + dative. Describes action started in past and still ongoing.

7. BIS (until)

   "Bis morgen" (until tomorrow)
   "Bis wann?" (Until when?)
   "Bis zum Ende" (until the end)
   "von ... bis" = "from ... to"
   "Von 9 bis 17 Uhr" (9 to 5)

   "bis" + accusative (when with article)

8. BEI (during / at)

   "Beim Essen" (during eating / at meals)
   "Beim Frühstück" (during breakfast)
   "Bei Nacht" (at night)
   "Beim Arbeiten" (during working)

   "bei" + dative. Means "during" or "in the context of."

9. WÄHREND (during — emphasises simultaneity)

   "Während des Essens" (during the meal)
   "Während der Arbeit" (during work)
   "Während des Films" (during the movie)

   "während" + genitive. More formal than "bei."

10. NACH / VOR pair (most-used prepositions of time):

    past: "vor drei Tagen" (3 days ago)
    past continuous: "seit drei Tagen" (for 3 days)
    past before: "bis vor drei Tagen" (until 3 days ago, exclusive)
    future: "in drei Tagen" (in 3 days)
    future ongoing: "für drei Tage" (for 3 days — limited)

CONTRACTIONS IN COMMON SPEECH
-------------------------------

Prepositions often contract with articles in spoken German:

  an + dem = am
  in + dem = im
  in + das = ins
  um + das = ums
  bei + dem = beim
  von + dem = vom
  zu + dem = zum
  zu + der = zur

This is used in ALL informal writing and conversation.

EXAMPLES IN CONTEXT
-------------------

Telling your schedule:
  "Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf."
  "Ich frühstücke um 7:30 Uhr."
  "Ich gehe um 8:30 Uhr zur Arbeit."
  "Ich arbeite von 9 bis 17 Uhr."
  "Ich esse um 12 Uhr zu Mittag."
  "Nach der Arbeit gehe ich nach Hause."
  "Vor dem Abendessen dusche ich."
  "Beim Essen höre ich Musik."

Talking about your past:
  "Vor drei Jahren habe ich angefangen, Deutsch zu lernen."
  "Seit einem Jahr bin ich in München."
  "Bis zum Ende des Monats muss ich den Bericht schreiben."

CULTURE NOTE
------------

In German letters/forms, you'll see:
  "München, den 15. März 2026"
This is the formal date format. The "den" is masculine accusative (for masculine months + masculine singular day). In spoken German, we just say "der fünfzehnte März."
