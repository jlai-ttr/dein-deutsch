THE GOLDEN RULE FOR REGULAR VERBS
---------------------------------
German regular verbs follow a predictable pattern:

1. Take the verb's INFINITIVE (basic form): e.g. "machen" (to do)
2. Remove the "-en" ending to get the STEM: "mach"
3. Add the correct ending based on the subject:

  ich          -e       = mache
  du           -st      = machst
  er/sie/es    -t       = macht
  wir          -en      = machen
  ihr          -t       = macht
  sie/Sie      -en      = machen

Same pattern for ALL regular verbs. Learn this once, use it forever.

VERB: MACHEN (TO DO / TO MAKE)
-------------------------------
  ich mache      = I do/make        = ISH MAH-kheh
  du machst      = you do/make (c)  = DOO MAHKHST
  er macht       = he does          = AIR MAHKHT
  sie macht      = she does         = ZEE MAKHHT
  es macht       = it does          = ESS MAKHHT
  wir machen     = we do            = VEER MAH-khen
  ihr macht      = you (pl) do      = EER MAKHHT
  sie machen     = they do          = ZEE MAH-khen
  Sie machen     = you (fml) do     = ZEE MAH-khen

Examples:
  "Ich mache meine Arbeit."   = I do my work.
  "Was machst du?"            = What are you doing? (casual)
  "Er macht das gut."         = He does that well.
  "Wir machen eine Pause."    = We take a break.

VERB: LERNEN (TO LEARN)
-----------------------
  ich lerne      = I learn
  du lernst      = you learn
  er lernt       = he learns
  wir lernen     = we learn
  ihr lernt      = you (pl) learn
  sie lernen     = they learn

  "Ich lerne Deutsch."        = I learn German.
  "Lernst du Deutsch?"        = Are you learning German? (casual)
  "Wir lernen viel."          = We learn a lot.

VERB: ARBEITEN (TO WORK)
------------------------
Note: stem is "arbeit-" (drop the -en, keep the t)
  ich arbeite    = I work           = ISH AR-by-teh
  du arbeitest   = you work
  er arbeitet    = he works
  wir arbeiten   = we work
  ihr arbeitet   = you (pl) work

  "Ich arbeite in Kuala Lumpur."
  = ISH AR-by-teh IN KWAH-lah LOOM-poor
  (I work in Kuala Lumpur.)

  "Wo arbeitest du?" = Where do you work?

VERB: SPRECHEN (TO SPEAK) — IRREGULAR STEM
-------------------------------------------
"Sprechen" drops the "e" in the stem for du/er forms:
  ich spreche    = I speak
  du sprichst    = you speak (note: sprichst, NOT sprechst)
  er spricht     = he speaks
  wir sprechen   = we speak

  "Ich spreche Englisch und Deutsch."
  = ISH SHPREH-kheh ENG-lish oont DOYTSH
  (I speak English and German.)

  "Sprechen Sie Deutsch?" = Do you speak German? (formal)

VERB: KOMMEN (TO COME)
----------------------
Also irregular stem (drop e for du/er):
  ich komme      = I come
  du kommst      = you come
  er kommt       = he comes
  wir kommen     = we come

  "Ich komme aus Malaysia." = I come from Malaysia.
  "Woher kommen Sie?"      = Where do you come from? (formal)
  "Er kommt aus Berlin."   = He comes from Berlin.

  Pronunciation: "kommen" = "KOM-men" (short o)

VERB: WOHNEN (TO LIVE / TO RESIDE)
-----------------------------------
  ich wohne      = I live
  du wohnst      = you live
  er wohnt       = he lives
  wir wohnen     = we live

  "Ich wohne in Kuala Lumpur." = I live in Kuala Lumpur.
  "Wo wohnen Sie?"             = Where do you live? (formal)

  Important: use "in" + city name. NO article. "Ich wohne in Berlin."

VERB: HEISSEN (TO BE CALLED) — REVIEW
---------------------------------------
  "Wie heißen Sie?" = VEE HAY-sen ZEE? (What is your name? — formal)
  "Ich heiße Jasper." = ISH HAY-seh Jasper.
  "Er heißt Henry."  = AIR HAYST HEN-ree.

VERB: GEHEN (TO GO) — IMPORTANT + IRREGULAR
--------------------------------------------
  ich gehe       = I go
  du gehst       = you go
  er geht        = he goes
  wir gehen      = we go

  "Ich gehe nach Hause."   = ISH GAY-eh NAHK HOW-zeh
    (I am going home.)
  "Wohin gehst du?"        = Where are you going?
  "Wir gehen ins Büro."    = We go to the office.

   a) ich __________    b) du __________
   c) er __________    d) wir __________

   a) I work in Kuala Lumpur.    = ________________
   b) He speaks English.         = ________________
   c) We learn German.           = ________________

ANSWERS
