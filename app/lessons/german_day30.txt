DAILY PRACTICE — REAL HABITS
------------------------------

To maintain your level and grow:

1. Read at least 30 minutes/day (slow is fine):
   - Easy readers (A1 graded)
   - Children's books (you'll find them surprisingly challenging at first)
   - News articles with dictionary lookup
   - Subtitles on German shows (start German, transition to no subtitles)

2. Listen to German podcasts:
   - Deutsche Welle Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten (slow news)
   - Coffee Break German
   - Slow German podcast
   - Easy German (YouTube with subtitles)

3. Speak SOMETHING every day:
   - Read aloud for 5 minutes
   - Narrate your actions out loud (in German)
   - Talk to yourself (yes, really — works)
   - Tandem partner (find one on Tandem app or HelloTalk)
   - Daily 5 min with native speaker (italki, $5-10/hr)

4. Write SOMETHING every day:
   - 1 paragraph about what you did
   - 1 email to yourself (don't send, but write)
   - A few sentences in a journal
   - Transcribe a podcast episode in German

5. Vocabulary review:
   - 10-20 minutes/day using Spaced Repetition (Anki, or here: /woerter)
   - Focus on words you actually USE
   - Don't try to memorize lists
